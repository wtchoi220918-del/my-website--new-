About Satoshi Tajiri

Satoshi Tajiri, is a video game designer and director. One famous game that he made was ,Pokemon. First Pokemon games that he made was Pokemon Red, Pokemon Green, and Pokemon Blue. He got his inspiration from old Japenese TV shows. When he was young he liked to collect bugs this also gave him a inspirtaion for his video game development.
